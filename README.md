## The poster

A newspaper front page, "The World in One Day — 22 November 2026", with six stories:

1. **Drones set a Russian cargo ship ablaze in the Baltic** — The Sparta II, bound for Kaliningrad, was hit by two drones in the Gulf of Finland on 21 November; its 16 crew evacuated as the burning ship drifted into Finnish waters. Finland says Ukraine was likely behind the attack.
2. **A 6.7 quake shakes Peru's Andes** — The tremor struck near Junín on Friday evening (USGS magnitude 6.7) and was felt in Lima; no casualties were reported.
3. **Climate talks stall in overtime** — COP31 negotiators in Antalya, Turkey, stretched past their Friday deadline, deadlocked over fossil fuels; the latest draft drops any mention of them, and a $120bn adaptation-finance target.
4. **Trump: Iran wants a deal he won't accept** — The US president demanded a "real deal" as Washington tightens its naval blockade and economic squeeze on Tehran.
5. **Denmark expels a Russian diplomat over a foiled plot** — Copenhagen blames Russian intelligence for a plot to kill US-Ukrainian rapper Kyivstoner at a September concert; two men are charged.
6. **Cyclone Akvan hits India's east coast** — The storm came ashore near Gopalpur, Odisha, on 20 November with winds gusting to 90 km/h; over 300,000 people were evacuated and no major damage was reported.

Sources: Reuters, AP, CNN, USGS, India Meteorological Department — 20–22 November 2026. All six facts were checked against those sources before the poster was drawn; no figures were invented.

## How it works

- `draw_poster.py` draws the poster locally with Pillow and saves `entry.png` (1024×1024).
- `main.py` is standard-library only: it just copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, so the program runs twice with byte-identical output and no network.

## Run it

```sh
python3 draw_poster.py
python3 main.py
```
